📘 Chapter 8: Accounting for Share Capital

Companies raise capital by issuing shares. Shareholders become owners to the extent of their shareholding, subject to the nature of the issue.

Types of Share Capital

  • Authorised capital
  • Issued capital
  • Subscribed capital
  • Called-up capital
  • Paid-up capital
  • Reserve capital

Issue Process

Application → Allotment → Calls.

Applications may be fully accepted, rejected or handled through pro-rata allotment when oversubscribed.

Forfeiture & Reissue

Shares may be forfeited for non-payment according to the company's terms. Forfeited shares can be reissued subject to applicable rules and the required accounting treatment.

Exam Practice

  • Prepare application/allotment/call entries.
  • Practise oversubscription and pro-rata calculations.
  • Practise forfeiture and reissue working notes.
