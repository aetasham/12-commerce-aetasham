📘 Chapter 10: Financial Statements of a Company

Financial statements present the financial performance and financial position of a company.

Statement of Profit and Loss

Shows revenue, expenses, profit before tax, tax and profit for the period according to the prescribed format.

Balance Sheet

Shows assets, equity and liabilities at a particular date. Items are presented under the prescribed headings and sub-headings.

Notes to Accounts

Notes provide additional details and disclosures supporting the financial statement figures.

Exam Focus

  • Learn headings and sub-headings.
  • Practise classification of items.
  • Practise adjustments and presentation.
