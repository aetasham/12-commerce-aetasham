📘 Chapter 5: Retirement of a Partner

Retirement is a reconstitution of partnership in which one existing partner leaves and the remaining partners continue the business.

Gaining Ratio

Gain = New Share − Old Share

The gaining partners compensate the retiring partner for goodwill according to the gaining ratio.

Adjustments

  • New profit-sharing ratio
  • Gaining ratio
  • Goodwill
  • Revaluation of assets and liabilities
  • Reserves and accumulated profits/losses
  • Retiring partner's capital/current account
  • Amount payable to retiring partner

Revaluation

Revaluation profit or loss is transferred to all old partners in old ratio before retirement.

Amount Due

Calculate capital, goodwill, reserves, revaluation and profit share, then deduct drawings and other debit balances as applicable.
