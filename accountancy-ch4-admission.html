📘 Chapter 4: Admission of a Partner

Admission occurs when a new partner enters an existing partnership with the consent of all existing partners.

Major Adjustments

  1. New profit-sharing ratio
  2. Sacrificing ratio
  3. Goodwill
  4. Revaluation of assets and liabilities
  5. Reserves and accumulated profits/losses
  6. Capital adjustment
  7. New Balance Sheet

Sacrificing Ratio

Sacrifice = Old Share − New Share

The new partner's share is generally sacrificed by existing partners.

Goodwill

Premium brought by the new partner for goodwill is credited to sacrificing partners in sacrificing ratio. If goodwill is adjusted through capital/current accounts, pass the required adjustment entry.

Revaluation

Increase in assets and decrease in liabilities create revaluation profit. Decrease in assets and increase in liabilities create revaluation loss. Profit/loss is transferred to old partners in old ratio.

Exam Checklist

  • Calculate new ratio first.
  • Calculate sacrificing ratio carefully.
  • Adjust goodwill before final capital balances.
  • Prepare Revaluation Account and revised Balance Sheet.
