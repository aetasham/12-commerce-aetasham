📘 Chapter 6: Death of a Partner

On death of a partner, the partnership is reconstituted and the deceased partner's dues are calculated for settlement with the legal representative or estate.

Amount Due

  • Capital balance
  • Share of goodwill
  • Share of revaluation profit/loss
  • Share of reserves
  • Profit up to date of death
  • Interest on capital, if applicable
  • Less drawings and other deductions

Profit till Death

Profit may be calculated on time basis or another basis specified by the question. If last year's profit is given, use the required fraction for the period elapsed.

Time-basis share = Previous Profit × Elapsed Period ÷ Previous Full Period

Settlement

The amount due may be transferred to the deceased partner's Executor's Account and later paid according to the terms.
