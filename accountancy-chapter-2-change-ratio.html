📗 Chapter 2: Change in Profit-Sharing Ratio

Concept

Existing partners may change their sharing ratio. The change affects goodwill, reserves and revaluation according to the terms of reconstitution.

Sacrificing Ratio = Old Share − New Share
Gaining Ratio = New Share − Old Share

Goodwill

A partner who sacrifices share should be compensated by the gaining partner. The adjustment is normally made in the gaining/sacrificing relationship specified by the question.

Revaluation

  • Increase in asset = profit.
  • Decrease in asset = loss.
  • Increase in liability = loss.
  • Decrease in liability = profit.

Reserves and Accumulated Profits

Existing reserves and accumulated profits/losses are normally distributed among old partners in the old ratio unless the question gives a different treatment.

Example

A and B share 3:2 and change to 1:1.

A old share 3/5, new share 1/2. A sacrifices 1/10. B gains 1/10. Therefore B compensates A for the goodwill adjustment.

Exam Tips

  1. Convert all ratios to fractions before subtracting.
  2. Check that the new ratio totals 1.
  3. Identify sacrificing and gaining partners separately.
  4. Do not mix old ratio with new ratio while distributing old reserves.
