📘 Chapter 3: Goodwill

Goodwill is the value of a firm's reputation, customer loyalty and other advantages that help it earn higher profits.

1. Need for Valuation

Goodwill is valued when there is admission, retirement, death, change in ratio or sale/reconstitution of a business.

2. Average Profit Method

Average Profit = Total Adjusted Profits ÷ Number of Years
Goodwill = Average Profit × Years' Purchase

Adjust abnormal gains/losses and non-recurring items before calculating average profit.

3. Super Profit Method

Normal Profit = Capital Employed × Normal Rate ÷ 100
Super Profit = Average Profit − Normal Profit
Goodwill = Super Profit × Years' Purchase

4. Capitalisation Method

Capitalised Value = Average Profit × 100 ÷ Normal Rate
Goodwill = Capitalised Value − Actual Capital Employed

Alternative: Capitalisation of Super Profit = Super Profit × 100 ÷ Normal Rate.

5. Exam Tips

  • Always adjust abnormal and non-recurring items.
  • Check the unit of capital employed and rate.
  • Write working notes clearly.
  • Use the method specified in the question.
