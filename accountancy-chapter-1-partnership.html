📘 Chapter 1: Partnership Fundamentals

Meaning

Partnership is a relationship between persons who agree to carry on a business and share its profits. Mutual agency is the key feature: each partner can act for the firm and bind the other partners.

Essential Features

  • Agreement between partners
  • Two or more persons
  • Lawful business
  • Profit-sharing
  • Mutual agency
  • Unlimited liability

Partnership Deed

A written agreement normally covers capital, profit ratio, drawings, interest on capital/drawings, salary or commission, admission, retirement and dispute terms.

In absence of agreement

  • Profits and losses are shared equally.
  • No interest on capital.
  • No interest on drawings.
  • Partner is generally not entitled to salary.
  • Interest on partner's loan is allowed at the applicable rate under the governing rules.

P&L Appropriation

It distributes the firm's net profit among partners after considering appropriations such as partner salary, commission and interest on capital, and credits income such as interest on drawings.

Partner share = Divisible profit × profit-sharing fraction

Capital Accounts

Fixed: capital remains fixed and routine adjustments go to Current Account. Fluctuating: profit, drawings, interest and other adjustments are made in Capital Account.

Exam checklist

  1. Read deed terms carefully.
  2. Separate business profit from appropriation.
  3. Apply ratio only after calculating divisible profit.
  4. Show working notes clearly.

Practice

A and B share profits 3:2. Divisible profit is ₹50,000. Calculate their shares.

Answer: A ₹30,000; B ₹20,000.
