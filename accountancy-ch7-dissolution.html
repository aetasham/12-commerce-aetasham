📘 Chapter 7: Dissolution of Partnership Firm

Dissolution means closing the business and settling the firm's assets, liabilities and partners' accounts.

Realisation Account

  • Transfer assets to Realisation Account as required.
  • Transfer external liabilities.
  • Record sale of assets.
  • Record payment of liabilities and realisation expenses.
  • Transfer profit/loss to partners in profit-sharing ratio.

Order of Settlement

  1. Pay outside liabilities.
  2. Pay partner's loan.
  3. Settle partners' capitals.
  4. Distribute remaining cash.

Important Point

Realisation profit is credited to partners' capital accounts; realisation loss is debited in their profit-sharing ratio.
