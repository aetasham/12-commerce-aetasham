📘 Accountancy Notes

Partnership

  • Profit sharing, goodwill, admission, retirement/death and dissolution.
  • Goodwill methods: average profit, super profit and capitalization.
  • Admission: adjust goodwill, revaluation, reserves and capitals.
  • Retirement/death: gaining ratio and settlement of outgoing partner.
  • Dissolution: Realisation Account and final settlement.

Company Accounts

  • Equity/preference shares, issue, forfeiture and reissue.
  • Important debenture accounting concepts.

Ratios & Cash Flow

  • Current ratio, quick ratio, debt-equity and profitability ratios.
  • Cash flow: operating, investing and financing activities.

💼 Business Studies Notes

Management

  • Functions: planning, organising, staffing, directing and controlling.
  • Objectives: organisational, social and personal.
  • Efficiency means minimum resource use; effectiveness means achieving objectives.

Principles & Environment

  • Fayol: division of work, discipline, unity of command and scalar chain.
  • Taylor: scientific management and standardisation.
  • Environment includes economic, social, technological, political and legal factors.

Finance & Marketing

  • Financial decisions: investment, financing and dividend.
  • Marketing mix: product, price, place and promotion.
  • Consumer rights include safety, information, choice and redressal.

📊 Economics Notes

National Income

  • GDP measures domestic production; GNP adds net factor income from abroad.
  • Methods: value added, income and expenditure.
  • Real GDP uses constant prices; nominal GDP uses current prices.

Money & Banking

  • Money functions as medium of exchange, measure of value and store of value.
  • Central bank conducts monetary policy and regulates banking.

Budget & BOP

  • Government budget contains estimated receipts and expenditure.
  • Fiscal deficit is excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings.
  • Balance of Payments records transactions with the rest of the world.

Indian Economic Development

  • Revise pre-reform economy, 1991 reforms and post-reform developments.
  • Key themes: human capital, rural development, employment, poverty and sustainable development.

💻 Informatics Practices Notes

Pandas

  • Series is one-dimensional labelled data; DataFrame is two-dimensional tabular data.
  • Know selection, filtering, sorting, adding and deleting data.

Visualization

  • Matplotlib creates line, bar and histogram charts.

SQL

  • SELECT retrieves; WHERE filters; ORDER BY sorts.
  • GROUP BY groups rows; COUNT, SUM, AVG, MIN and MAX are aggregate functions.

Networks & Cyber Safety

  • Revise LAN, MAN, WAN, devices, protocols and internet concepts.
  • Digital footprints, cyber safety, intellectual property and ethical technology use.
