📘 Chapter 11: Analysis of Financial Statements

Analysis interprets financial statements to understand liquidity, solvency, profitability and efficiency.

Comparative Statements

Figures of two or more periods are compared. Calculate absolute change and percentage change where required.

Common-size Statements

Each item is expressed as a percentage of a common base, helping comparison across periods or companies.

Ratio Analysis

Ratios establish relationships between financial statement figures. Main groups include liquidity, solvency, activity and profitability ratios.

Interpretation

A ratio should be interpreted with its trend, industry context and other financial information; a single ratio is not enough for a complete conclusion.
