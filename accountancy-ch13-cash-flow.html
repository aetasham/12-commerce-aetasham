📘 Chapter 13: Cash Flow Statement

Cash Flow Statement explains inflows and outflows of cash and cash equivalents during an accounting period.

Operating Activities

Main revenue-generating activities of the business, such as cash received from customers and cash paid for operating expenses.

Investing Activities

Purchase and sale of long-term assets and investments. Examples include machinery purchased and fixed assets sold.

Financing Activities

Activities changing share capital and borrowings, such as issue of shares, raising/repaying loans and other financing flows according to the applicable classification.

Indirect Method

Start with accounting profit, adjust for non-cash and non-operating items, and account for working-capital changes to arrive at cash from operating activities.

Operating Cash Flow = Adjusted Profit ± Working Capital Changes (with required adjustments)

Exam Tips

  • Classify every transaction into operating, investing or financing.
  • Do not treat non-cash transactions as cash flows.
  • Show working notes clearly.
