📘 ACCOUNTANCY

Q1. Partnership kya hai?
Ans. Partnership do ya do se adhik vyaktiyon ke beech agreement ke madhyam se business chalane aur profits share karne ka sambandh hai. Partners ki mutual agency partnership ki important feature hai.
Q2. Partnership Deed kya hoti hai?
Ans. Partnership deed written agreement hoti hai jisme capital, profit-sharing ratio, drawings, interest, salary, commission aur anya terms record ki jaati hain.
Q3. Goodwill kya hai?
Ans. Goodwill business ki reputation aur anya advantages ka value hai jo firm ko normal se adhik profit earn karne mein help karte hain.
Q4. Average Profit Method ka formula likhiye.
Ans. Goodwill = Average Profit × Number of Years' Purchase.
Q5. Sacrificing Ratio kya hai?
Ans. Sacrificing Ratio batata hai ki existing partners ne new partner ke favour mein kitna profit share sacrifice kiya. Formula: Old Ratio − New Ratio.
Q6. Admission of Partner mein main adjustments kaun se hote hain?
Ans. New ratio, sacrificing ratio, goodwill, revaluation of assets/liabilities, reserves/accumulated profits or losses aur partners' capitals adjust kiye jaate hain.
Q7. Retirement mein Gaining Ratio kya hai?
Ans. Gaining Ratio = New Share − Old Share. Iska use retiring partner ki goodwill adjustment mein kiya jaata hai.
Q8. Dissolution mein Realisation Account ka purpose kya hai?
Ans. Realisation Account assets/liabilities ke settlement aur dissolution par hone wale profit ya loss ko determine karne ke liye banaya jaata hai.
Q9. Current Ratio ka formula?
Ans. Current Ratio = Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities.
Q10. Quick Ratio ka formula?
Ans. Quick Ratio = Quick Assets ÷ Current Liabilities.
Q11. Cash Flow Statement ke teen activities kaun si hain?
Ans. Operating Activities, Investing Activities aur Financing Activities.
Q12. Numerical: Average Profit ₹60,000 aur years' purchase 3 hai. Goodwill?
Ans. ₹60,000 × 3 = ₹1,80,000.

💼 BUSINESS STUDIES

Q1. Management kya hai?
Ans. Management planning, organising, staffing, directing aur controlling ka process hai jisse organisational objectives effectively aur efficiently achieve kiye jaate hain.
Q2. Management ke objectives kya hain?
Ans. Organisational objectives, social objectives aur personal objectives.
Q3. Coordination kya hai?
Ans. Coordination individual aur departmental efforts ko integrate karta hai taaki organisation ke common objectives efficiently achieve ho saken.
Q4. Fayol ke koi chaar principles likhiye.
Ans. Division of Work, Authority and Responsibility, Discipline, Unity of Command. Iske alawa Unity of Direction, Equity, Order, Initiative etc. bhi important hain.
Q5. Planning kya hai?
Ans. Planning ka meaning hai pehle se decide karna ki kya karna hai, kaise karna hai, kab karna hai aur kisne karna hai.
Q6. Delegation ke elements kya hain?
Ans. Authority, Responsibility aur Accountability.
Q7. Staffing process ke major steps likhiye.
Ans. Manpower planning, recruitment, selection, placement/orientation, training and development, performance appraisal, promotion/career planning aur compensation.
Q8. Directing ke elements kya hain?
Ans. Supervision, Motivation, Leadership aur Communication.
Q9. Controlling process ke steps?
Ans. Standards set karna, actual performance measure karna, comparison karna, deviations analyse karna aur corrective action lena.
Q10. Marketing Mix ke 4 Ps kya hain?
Ans. Product, Price, Place aur Promotion.
Q11. Consumer ke six major rights likhiye.
Ans. Right to Safety, Information, Choice, Being Heard, Redressal aur Consumer Education.
Q12. Case Study: Manager employees ko bonus aur recognition deta hai. Kaunsa concept?
Ans. Ye Motivation ka application hai. Bonus financial incentive aur recognition non-financial incentive ka example hai.

📊 ECONOMICS

Q1. GDP kya hai?
Ans. GDP ek period mein domestic territory ke andar produced final goods and services ki monetary value ko measure karta hai.
Q2. Money ke functions likhiye.
Ans. Medium of exchange, measure of value, store of value aur standard of deferred payments.
Q3. Commercial Bank kya karta hai?
Ans. Commercial banks deposits accept karte hain aur loans/advances provide karte hain. Ye payment aur banking services bhi dete hain.
Q4. Multiplier ka simple formula?
Ans. Multiplier = 1 ÷ (1 − MPC), simple model mein.
Q5. Government Budget kya hai?
Ans. Government budget ek financial year ke estimated receipts aur expenditure ka annual statement hai.
Q6. Fiscal Deficit ka formula?
Ans. Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure − Total Receipts excluding borrowings.
Q7. Balance of Payments kya record karta hai?
Ans. BOP residents aur rest of the world ke beech economic transactions ko record karta hai.
Q8. Liberalisation, Privatisation aur Globalisation ko collectively kya kaha jaata hai?
Ans. LPG reforms. India mein major economic reforms 1991 se implement hue.
Q9. Human Capital kya hai?
Ans. Human capital people mein embodied knowledge, skills aur health ka stock hai jo productivity aur development ko improve karta hai.
Q10. Sustainable Development kya hai?
Ans. Aisa development jo present needs ko meet kare bina future generations ki needs meet karne ki ability ko compromise kiye.
Q11. Numerical: MPC = 0.75. Simple multiplier?
Ans. 1 ÷ (1 − 0.75) = 1 ÷ 0.25 = 4.
Q12. Poverty ke do causes?
Ans. Unemployment, low income, unequal opportunities aur education/health tak inadequate access jaise factors poverty ko badha sakte hain.

💻 INFORMATICS PRACTICES

Q1. Pandas Series kya hai?
Ans. Series Pandas ki one-dimensional labelled data structure hai.
Q2. DataFrame kya hai?
Ans. DataFrame two-dimensional labelled tabular data structure hai jisme rows aur columns hote hain.
Q3. SQL ka full form?
Ans. Structured Query Language.
Q4. SELECT command ka use?
Ans. SELECT database table se required data retrieve karne ke liye use hota hai.
Q5. WHERE clause ka use?
Ans. WHERE records ko condition ke basis par filter karta hai.
Q6. GROUP BY kya karta hai?
Ans. GROUP BY similar values wali rows ko groups mein arrange karta hai, commonly aggregate functions ke saath.
Q7. Primary Key kya hai?
Ans. Primary key table ke records ko uniquely identify karti hai aur duplicate/null identification ko prevent karne mein help karti hai.
Q8. LAN, MAN aur WAN ke full forms?
Ans. LAN = Local Area Network, MAN = Metropolitan Area Network, WAN = Wide Area Network.
Q9. HTTPS kya hai?
Ans. HTTPS = HyperText Transfer Protocol Secure. Ye web communication ko secure karne ke liye encryption use karta hai.
Q10. Cyber safety ke teen measures?
Ans. Strong unique passwords use karo, suspicious links se bacho aur important data ka backup rakho.
Q11. Bar chart kab useful hai?
Ans. Different categories ke values ko compare karne ke liye bar chart useful hota hai.
Q12. MCQ: SQL mein records filter karne ke liye kaunsa clause? A) ORDER BY B) WHERE C) GROUP BY D) SELECT
Ans. B) WHERE.
