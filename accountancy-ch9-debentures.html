📘 Chapter 9: Issue of Debentures

A debenture is a long-term debt instrument. Debenture holders are creditors of the company.

Issue

  • At par
  • At premium
  • At discount where permitted by the applicable rules
  • For consideration other than cash
  • As collateral security

Interest

Debenture interest is a finance cost. It is payable according to the terms of issue.

Redemption

Redemption means repayment of the debenture liability. Practise journal entries and the required calculations for the redemption method given in the question.
